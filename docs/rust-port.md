# Rust port: crates and status

_Started 2026-10-03. Plan and decisions: `docs/project-goal.md`. Ledger track `rust`._

## Crates (`gow2-rs/crates`)

| Crate | Engine types? | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `gow2-formats` | no | ISO, TOC, WAD, GFX/PAL, textures, MDL parts, rigs, clip channels (ports of the Python tools, tested against them) |
| `gow2-skel` | no | skeleton, clip baking and sampling, pose blending, two-joint skinning, joint-local parts |
| `gow2-kratos` | no | controller logic ported from the decompiled game: ground locomotion, animation selection |
| `gow2-bevy` | Bevy 0.19.1 | window, input, rendering; binaries `kratos-view` (clip viewer) and `kratos-play` (walk around) |

Everything is read from the user's own WAD at start-up. Bevy is replaceable: no other crate imports it.

## Controller status (`gow2-kratos`)

Ported from `docs/character-update.md` section 6 (ground handler `FUN_002284c8`):
- speed profile CONFIRMED from RAM: minimum 0.9, target 7.5 m/s, acceleration and deceleration 50 m/s^2, turn rate 0.25 per 1/60 s; 16 units per metre;
- `FUN_00221ad8` speed approach (target below 0.0001 becomes 0, otherwise at least the minimum), `FUN_00221be0` turn (an exponential approach: heading += wrapped angle x rate x dt x 60), `FUN_0021afa8` friction with no stick;
- fixed tick 59.94 Hz.

Jump (added 2026-10-03, `docs/character-update.md` section 8, tuning CONFIRMED from RAM): launch 15.25 m/s, gravity 50 m/s^2, the rise turns into a fall when the vertical speed drops below 2 m/s, a double jump (vertical speed zeroed, then 15.25 m/s added, once per air time, window -100 < vy < 20 m/s), terminal speed 50 m/s, air acceleration 7.5 m/s^2, landing on a flat plane. Clips follow the move table: `navJump` / `navJumpUp` (horizontal speed within 1 m/s), `navDoubleJump`, `navFall` / `navFallLoop` (below -40 m/s), `navLand`. Tests: apex 36.7 units (the documented 2.3 m), landing 0.5 to 0.8 s after take-off, one double jump only.

Not yet ported: ledge/ladder/climb states (the sheet has `Ladder`, `Climbable`, `ClimbGuide` and `NoWallHang` surfaces now), the camera zones, the move graph (combat), the exact `walkBlend` node, coyote time (needs a ledge), the move-system gravity scale.

Approximate: the air steering (the game uses `tuning+0x8c` times a ramp `+0x350`; here it is the turn-rate factor 0.05 in the air, LOW); the air fade time (0.08 s, LOW); landing ends after the clip or once Kratos moves off (guess).

Open or approximate: the ground friction coefficient comes from the ground collider material and is not decoded (default chosen to match the documented 0.15 s stop, LOW); the walk blend is a speed mix of `navWalkSlow` and `navWalkFast` (MEDIUM); fade times between idle and walk are guesses (LOW).

Corroboration: the planted-foot speeds measured from the clips are `navWalkSlow` 1.32 m/s and `navWalkFast` 7.15 m/s, which span the decoded profile (minimum 0.9, target 7.5 m/s), as a speed-driven blend of those two clips should.

## Levels (added 2026-10-03)

`gow2-formats::level` ports the placement rules of `tools/gltf_export.py` (`docs/models.md`): ref instances (a type `0x00020001` record names a node, a following model reference binds it; vertex world position `(p * s + o) * R + W`, `o` and `s` from the model's own 88-byte record), models drawn once at their model-record position, and **rigged models in bind pose through their `go` node** (rotation rows at `+0x20`, translation at `+0x44` of the 104-byte record; the vertices are joint-local, so the rig's bind joint matrices, which carry the model's own offset and scale, come first). Test against the Python report for RHOD10: 38 instances, 7 bound models, 25 rigged drawable models.

`gow2-bevy::level` draws them merged per material (textures and the MAT blend byte: additive and subtractive handled, alpha cut-out when the texture has transparency). RHOD10: 91 placed models, 85,696 triangles. Animated objects (doors, breakable props, chunks) are shown in their bind pose and do not move.

**Floor.** ~~The game's static world collision data is not decoded, so Kratos's floor comes from the level's render triangles (`TriWorld`): a stand-in (LOW).~~ Superseded 2026-10-03: the level's real collision is the `RIB_sheet` record (`docs/collision.md` section 3). `gow2_formats::sheet` reads it; `gow2_bevy::level` keeps the polygons the hero's queries do not skip (floor probes skip `0x10042`, horizontal sweeps `0x41010042`, plus the hero's own `NoPlayerCollision`) and passes them as oriented triangles to `gow2_kratos::world::CollisionWorld`. `TriWorld` remains the fallback for a level without a sheet. He follows slopes and steps (up 10, down 14 units: LOW), walks off ledges into a fall (no double jump, as `EnterFall` mode 2), and lands on the first floor below. The hall floor is in `grandHall`/`firstRoom`, which carry a skeleton and are placed through `go` nodes.

**Walls and ceilings.** `World::push_out` samples the body (radius 0.6 m, height 2.2 m from the tuning, MEDIUM) with spheres between the step height and the head, takes the closest point on each nearby wall triangle, and slides the body out horizontally (up to three passes, as the game's move loop). Contacts below the step height are kerbs and are ignored on the ground. A rising body that meets a ceiling surface stops at it and starts to fall. Press F2 in `kratos-play` to draw the polygons near Kratos (green floors, blue ceilings, orange walls, grey skipped surfaces).

**Start.** For `RHOD10` he starts where the RAM captures put him: world (-1704.28, 3712.00, -5353.45), heading 0.586 (the game's yaw matrix, `docs/animation.md` ground truth).

## Combat (added 2026-10-04)

Crates: `gow2-formats::dc` reads the hero's gameplay data (1,041 moves, branches, hit windows, actions, the entry list; tested against
the Python reader). `gow2-kratos::moves` is the move system (`docs/combat.md` section 11): entry branches, per-move branch evaluation,
the queue and dequeue, move start, hit windows and the per-move list of hits dealt. `gow2-kratos::combat` resolves open windows against
victims: damage = window damage x multiplier, knock-back = the ground or air impulse x 16 / mass, once per (victim, window, sub-hit),
the on-hit actions one frame later, meters (health, magic, god) and the hit pause the move stored.

`kratos-play` uses it: Square (J, West) starts the combo from the entry list and taps inside the windows chain it (five slashes, then
the finisher); Triangle (K, North) starts the slam; Circle (L, East); block (Q, R1) is the parry; magic (E, L1) has no spells yet. Three
dummies stand in front of Kratos with 100 health each: they flash red, slide back and get up after 2 s; the title shows the move, its
time, the dummy health, hits and the god meter. A hit freezes everything for the move's stored hit pause. `GOW_DEMO=1` taps Square on a
timer for a self-check.

**Blades (added 2026-10-04).** `gow2-formats::dc` reads the attachment records (`ATT_Chains` Ã¢â€ â€™ two `tChained`: object, hand, free and
stowed joint names, 0.35 m snap, chain joints; test against the names in `docs/animation.md`). `gow2-kratos::blades` is the engine-free
logic: each frame the distance rule picks back, hand or free mode from the world positions of the three slot joints, the mode is kept
during a stance (`MOV+4 & 2`), and the blade's matrix follows the slot joint (snapping on the back and in the hand, lagging by
`0.4 x dt x 60` in the free mode). CONFIRMED against RAM: with the blades stowed, each blade object's matrix (`object + 0x20`) is bit for
bit the world matrix of `leftBladeBack`/`rightBladeBack` (`analysis/runtime/ingame2`), so there is no extra offset. `gow2-bevy::blades`
loads `MDL_MAIblade` from `R_WEAPON0_5.WAD` (the stage the RAM captures show), puts the vertices through the rig's 1/64 bind matrix, shows
the same mesh at both hands. While a blade is out the chain is drawn as 69 textured link quads (`MAT_chainlink`) alternating between two perpendicular strips along a sagging curve from `LChain`/`RChain` to the blade (the game's own link placement is not decoded, MEDIUM), and a trail follows it as `docs/effects.md` 5.1 describes (160-sample ring, 4 sub-samples a frame, 0.32 s lifetime, alpha fading with age, `MAT_swordtrail`, edge points at the tip and 80 % along the blade). In play the
blades sit crossed on the back while walking, go to the hands for the first slash and fly out on their chains in the finisher; the title
shows the mode of each. The blades are stepped in the fixed tick, so the hit tests use the pose the player sees. The default blades have no forced attachment mode (`tActionForceAttachment` forces only the hand-held item for
them), so the distance rule alone drives them.

**Concussions** (the slam's ground smash has no hit window): a `SCR_Concussion` action spawns a growing sphere at the clip's `zeroJoint`/`synchJoint` marker, 0.1 s, radius to 32 units, damage 6 once per victim; shapes other than the sphere are not ported (`docs/combat.md` section 13). **Attack volumes are the game's collision balls** (`docs/combat.md` section 12): the blades' 13.2-unit balls, fists, feet, body and throw ball at their joints, selected by the hit window's volume id and swept over each tick; F3 draws them. Not yet: the blades' own animation, fire effects and lights, the trail colour table, evades (the
right stick drives the camera), block and parry flow on the receiving side, enemy AI and attacks, hits on Kratos, death, magic, the
air-float velocity scale, slow motion on parry (the slowdown action is wired but unused), camera shake and rumble, sounds and effects.
The stance exit is a stand-in (leaves on walking or after 4 s).

## Playing it

## Level and camera fixes (2026-10-04)

- **Mesh record names are cut to 19 characters.** The mesh of model `<name>` is stored as `MDL_<name>_0`, but a longer string keeps only its last 19 characters
  (`MDL_storageRoomMain_0` -> `L_storageRoomMain_0`, `MDL_ballistaPlatform_0` -> `_ballistaPlatform_0`); the header record `MDL_<name>` keeps its full name. The loaders matched
  the full string, so 12 models of RHOD10 were never drawn (the storage room, ballista platform and pults, the broken-wall group ... which also left holes in the floor of the
  hall). `gow2_formats::wad::mesh_record_name` is the one place that builds the key; `level::parse` and `mdl::model_materials` use it. The Python report has the same blind spot
  (25 rigged drawable models; with the fix 37 of 82). Textures and materials are not affected (23-character names).
- **Camera.** It starts behind Kratos, swings round behind him while he walks and the player is not steering (1.5 s after the last manual input; `GOW_NOAUTOCAM=1` turns it off), and
  it is stopped by both the collision polygons and the opaque render geometry (columns and walls that have no collision polygon), never closer than 14 units to him.
- **Debug aids in `kratos-play`:** `N` (or `GOW_NOCLIP=1`) walks through walls and keeps the height over gaps; `GOW_TOUR="secs:x:y[:j];..."` scripts the stick; `GOW_CAM=yaw,pitch,dist`,
  `GOW_NOCAMCOL=1`, `GOW_OVERLAY=1`, `GOW_MATLIST=1`, `GOW_MODELLIST=1`; falling 2,500 units below the start respawns him.
- **Tools.** `tools/collision_map.py` (top-down PNG of a level's collision sheet, crop, height band, surface highlight); `gow2-kratos` example `reach` (flood fill of what the controller can
  walk to from the start, with a probe of why it stops); `gow2-formats` example `sheet_probe` (surfaces near a point).
- **What RHOD10 allows.** From the start the corridor and the storage room beside it are reachable (21 % of the floor, 53 % if the rock walls are ignored): the way on to the grand
  hall is closed by solid rock-wall polygons, so it opens by a script or event in the game (the gate models and door animations are not played). Use `N` to look at the rest.

## Model audit (2026-10-04, third playtest: "the model looks broken, you filled Kratos, check the models, weapons and magic")
What was checked, and what it showed.
- **Kratos's mesh decode: correct. CONFIRMED.** (1) The triangle rule (a strip vertex with bit 15 of `w` set draws no triangle) gives the fewest open edges of the three rules tried; the head part welds into a closed surface with 0 open edges (`examples/mdl_open`). (2) The part headers (`+0x1c`, high half) hold the number of distinct vertices, which equals the decoded count for the small parts and the decoded count minus the 2-vertex overlap per batch for the large ones. (3) No vertex is left without a triangle. (4) `R_HERO01` holds a single mesh, no second layer.
- **Skinning formula: CONFIRMED from the VU1 code** (`analysis/vu`, program B at 0x440 and the fragment for variant 0x7b). The second matrix is built as `(M_second - M_first) * (-1/512)` and the vertex is `M_first(p) + w * M2'(p)` with `w = (2 * w15) / 16`, so the result is `P_first + (w15 / 4096) * (P_second - P_first)`: exactly `gow2_skel::skin_positions_blend`. The second joint is `(w >> 12) & 0xf` of the tag word (the VU rounds `w / 1024`), the first is `(w & 0x3ff) / 4`. The weights are 0 to 4096 in steps of about 0.1 (`examples/mdl_weights`).
- **Vertex colour: CONFIRMED.** The fragments of the character variants (0x7b, 0x3b) copy the baked vertex colour through `colour = vf31 + vf30 * colour` clamped to 255; there is no lighting from the normals in them, so the dark inside of the armour is the game's colour too (the game adds the per-object ambient `vf31`, which `INNER_LIGHT` approximates). The material tint `MAT +0x60..+0x6c` is the per-object colour `vf30`: the loader now applies it (the Medusa beam is (0.75, 0.28, 0.24), the tornado (0.73, 0.82, 0.91), the Bone staff's crystal (0.97, 0, 1), the bare Kratos's skin 1.5). Vertex colours of weapons and effect models are no longer clamped to 128 (the GS modulation goes up to 2.0).
- **The "hole in the back" is the Blades of Chaos, not Kratos.** With `GOW_NOBLADES=1` the armoured back is a closed plate; the black round spot belongs to the stowed blade model (its chain eyelet). The fillers (`hero::FILLER`) were therefore never needed and showed through as a brown patch behind that eyelet; they are removed from the default (`GOW_FILL=1` brings them back).
- **Not an error but looks odd: the skin at the neck.** In `navCombatIdle` about 20 triangle edges between neck and head shrink to a tenth of their length: neighbouring vertices have second-joint weights 0.2 and 0.75 and the two joints are turned 20 to 35 degrees against each other. With the formula confirmed this is the data, not a decode fault (`examples/skin_stretch`, `clip_scales`).
- **Effect models are real now.** `WindChargeGust` (204 long along -z, 22 to 30 across), `WindTornado` (about 100 across, 75 tall), `WindTempest` (676 across, 445 tall), `MedusaBeam` (402 long along -z), `medusaBomb` (16 across: the 88-byte record's scale 128 divides the vertices) and the two bolt models `lghtnSingleBolt` and `electricBolt` (29 joints, 233 units down -y) are drawn with their own clip and material instead of simple shapes (`GOW_FXPROC=1` brings the shapes back). The model files record names are cut to their last 19 characters (`wad::mesh_record_name`); `spawn_hero` used the uncut name and panicked for `WindChargeGust`. Still shapes: the Earth blasts (`earthStomp` is decoded: 33 to 250 units wide, 0.63 s) and the Electric core. The models' sizes differ from the stand-in radii of `magic.rs` (tornado matches at 2.5 m = 50 units; the tempest model is 338 units in radius against the 120 used for its damage); the visual is scaled to the damage radius.

## Magic system and effects (2026-10-04)
`gow2-kratos::magic` (no engine types) implements the five magics from the move data's script classes (`docs/magic.md` section 9); `gow2-bevy::fx` draws the result with the effect models (see "Model audit"), simple shapes for the rest. `kratos-play`: Tab or d-pad cycles the magic, L1 / E casts, then Square, Triangle and Circle choose the Medusa beam, flash and bomb, or the Wind gust, tornado and tempest; Lightning and Electric cast straight; Earth chains its moves by the upgrade level. `GOW_MAGIC=<id>` starts with one selected, `GOW_MAGIC_LEVEL=0..2`, `GOW_LOGMAGIC=1` logs the scripts, `GOW_FXTEST=beam|bomb|gust|tornado|tempest|rock|blast|bolt|core` shows one effect, `GOW_CAMBEHIND=<rad>` keeps the camera behind the heading. Kratos's pose while aiming is the full-body stance with the three aim clips over it (see the magic doc); the Medusa head follows the right hand.

## Sub-weapons (2026-10-04)
`gow2-bevy::subweapon` shows the three sub-weapons the data calls Bone, Hammer and Olympus (moves `MOV_BoneSquare01` ..., attachments `ATT_Bone` ..., objects `bone`, `hammer`, `olympus`; no retail names are assigned, the sound names suggest the Bone object is a staff: `SND_STAFFWHSH`). Press B in `kratos-play` to cycle none, Bone, Hammer, Olympus.
- **Clips.** `R_S_BONE0`, `R_S_HAMMER0` and `R_S_OLYMPUS0` each carry `ANM_Hero_Weapon*` with the clips Kratos plays while holding the weapon (22, 22 and 27 of them decode against the hero rig: `wpnBoneSlash01`, `wpnHammerSlam01`, `wpnOlympusSpecialBolt` ...). While a weapon is held, Kratos's locomotion clips are renamed to the weapon's (`navIdle` becomes `wpnBoneIdle`, `navWalkSlow` becomes `wpnBoneWalkSlow`, ...); the moves are chosen by `Progress::sub_weapon` through the existing `sub_weapon_ok` gating (HIGH, `docs/combat.md`). The chain blades are hidden while one is held and the weapon is hidden during magic (`SCR_HideSubWeapon`).
- **Objects.** Each WAD has the weapon's model: `bone` has a 12-joint rig and its own animation with Kratos's clip names, `hammer` and `olympus` are rigid (Olympus has its scale, 1/32, in the root joint). The attachment records give `RWeapIH` for both slots with snap 0, so the weapon sits at that joint's matrix (as the blades do). Every non-hero rigged model has joint-local vertices (`hero::spawn_hero` now treats it so; Kratos's own model keeps the `local_parts` heuristic).
- **Attack volumes.** `CDV_gobone` (13 balls), `CDV_gohammer` (5), `CDV_goolympus` (4): the material ids 12, 11 and 10 are the volume ids the moves' hit windows select. The balls are placed through the weapon's pose and the hand matrix and join the attacker's balls (`SubWeapon::balls`); the hits, hit stop, shake and combo counter work as for the blades.
- **Sound.** The `SBP_*` banks of the sub-weapon and magic WADs are loaded with the shared ones.
- **Not done:** the weapons' own effects (Bone's shard and poison, Hammer's soul, Olympus's bolt and sphere: the `R_S_*` WADs have the models and particle records), the upgrade levels (the `1` and `2` WADs; only level 0 is loaded), pickups, the equip menu, the special moves' costs.

## Fixes after the fourth playtest, and the Rhodes soldiers (2026-10-05)
The user said the Medusa head's tentacles look deformed, the magic effects and the blade flames look wrong, and asked for enemies.
- **Which non-hero models are skinned in model space. HIGH.** The earlier rule treated every non-hero rigged model as joint-local (each vertex relative to its joint, placed by that joint's world matrix). That is right for `olympus`, `hammer`, `earthRock` and `lghtnRadius` but wrong for models that are skinned meshes in model space like Kratos: the Medusa head (24 joints), `bone`, the bolts, `windBow`, the gust, tornado, tempest, `MedusaBeam` and `earthStomp`. For the head it made the snakes long spikes. `hero::spawn_hero` now decides by comparing the bounding diagonal of the raw vertices with that of the vertices placed by their joints: if the raw one is not larger, the model is model-space skinned. `GOW_LOCALPARTS=0|1` overrides, `GOW_LOGFX=1` logs the decision. After the change the head reads as a head (face, compact hair) and the effect models still look as before.
- **MAT tint is not applied to additive materials. HIGH.** The blade's face texture (`MAT_blade3b`) has its cyan strokes in the additive layer; the tint (0.7, 0, 0) of the material turned them red-brown. Additive materials (blend byte 0x48) now ignore the tint; the strokes show as pale cyan on the bronze body. Not animated yet (`ANM_blade3b` slot 0 pulses the layer).
- **Wind magic.** The plain blast sphere that stood in for `SCR_WindBlowHit` (a solid grey dome) is gone: the blow now fires `gowindblowhit`, and a gust that hits a victim fires `gowindgusthit` (both groups of `R_M_WIND0`, `docs/particles.md` section 7). All blast spheres are only drawn with `GOW_FXPROC=1`. Not wired: `gowindgustchargeup`.
- **Rhodes soldiers (new).** `gow2-bevy::soldier` loads `R_RHSOLD00.WAD` (model `rhsold00`, 18 joints, built of rigid joint-local pieces; the 54-clip bank `ANM_rhsold00` has `navIdle`, `navWalkF`, `attCharge`, `attChargeRun`, `attChargeslice`, `hitFront`/`Back`/`Left`/`Right`/`Stagger`/`Air`/`Launch`/`Land`/`GetUp`, `death00..06`, `taunt01/02`, `csSoldirKill`, `csSoldirGrappleX`, ...; list them with `gow2-formats` example `clip_list`). They replace the three grey capsules of the training area (`GOW_CAPSULES=1` brings the capsules back). The model looks along -z like Kratos, so it is drawn with the sim heading as it is (a half turn added first was a mistake: the soldiers walked facing away from Kratos). Which clip plays is chosen from the victim and its brain: `navIdle` standing, `navWalkF` approaching, `attChargeslice` spread over the brain's wind-up and recovery, `hitFront` or `hitStagger` (when the push is over 60 units/s) on a hit, `hitLaunch`/`hitAir`/`hitLand`/`hitGetUp` while thrown, one of `death00/04/06` when dead; clips cross-fade over 0.15 s. They turn to face Kratos, flash red when hit and turn grey when petrified. The soldiers fight back by default (the simple brain of `gow2_kratos::enemy`; `V` toggles, `GOW_PASSIVE=1` starts passive). **The soldiers' own behaviour (`BHV_`, `SCR_`) is not decoded, so which clip fits which blow, the clip speeds and the timing of the strike inside `attChargeslice` are guesses (LOW).** Overlapping victims are pushed apart. `GOW_KILLENEMY=<s>` kills the first soldier and throws the second at that time, for looking at the death and launch clips.

### Widescreen HUD and menu text (2026-10-05)
The movie's stage is 4:3 (640 x 480); the HUD used to sit in a 4:3 box in the middle of a 16:9 window (the pause backdrop was a dark 4:3 rectangle). `hud::Hud::widen` now lays the pieces out across the window: a piece as wide as the stage (the backdrop) is stretched to the full width, a piece whose edge is more than 30 px clear of the centre moves out to the left or right edge by half the extra width (the health sword and orb counter go to the left corner, the hit counter and helmet icon to the right), and everything else, including the Omega emblem and the menu items, stays centred. This is a layout choice, not the game's (the PS2 game squeezes the whole 4:3 frame in its widescreen mode, LOW what it does to the HUD). **Text boxes: the second value of the text field record (`raw[26..28]`) is the box width, not its right edge** (MEDIUM: read as a width the centred menu items, the "Pause" title and "YOU ARE DEAD" all sit on the stage centre; read as an edge they sat 20 to 130 px right of it). `GOW_LOGTEXT=1` prints every text field's box and placement.

### Upgrade menu, per-clip variables, selection label (2026-10-05)
- **The upgrade menu** is `PowerUpMenu` of `FLP_HUDA` (opened with `PS2_PSMMenu_Event` = 1; the pause menu is the same movie). In `kratos-play`: **U or the Select/Create button** opens it, up/down (d-pad, arrows, stick flick) move the highlight through the items (`PressUp`/`PressDown`), R1/L1 (E/Q) are `PressRight`/`PressLeft`, **Cross or Enter held** drains red orbs into the highlighted item (the engine sets `PauseXButton_Check` = 1 and plays `PowerUpMenu:PowerStart`; the movie counts `PS2_PowerOrb_Count` down and raises `PS2_<item>_Level`), Circle/Backspace/Esc/U closes. The engine reads the orbs and levels back every tick (`hud_feed`); magic levels raise `MagicSys::level` (0 to 2). When the movie ends it reports `PS2_PSMDone` and the menu closes. The data it needs from the engine: `PS2_<Lightning|Medusa|Wind|Electric|Earth|Hammer|Bone|Olympus>_Status` (1 = owned), `_Level`, `<name>Orb_Count`, `PS2_Blades_Level`, `PS2_BladesOrb_Count` (read from `PowerUpMenu` frames 5 and 23 to 29, `examples/flp_actions`). **Not done:** the level-up splash is not seen (the movie moves on at once), the medallions of the magics look dark, costs shown come from the message table (`PS2_3900`..`3981`, orbs per level; unchecked against play), the real orb levels per item are not kept between openings (every item starts at the magic level, no orbs in it), blades levels are not used by the controller. `GOW_UPGRADE=<s>`, `GOW_UPSEQ="2:down,3:x,9:xup"`, `GOW_ORBS=<n>`, `GOW_LOGMENU=1` test it without input.
- **Movie variables are per clip now (`flp_play`).** `x` in a clip's action is that clip's own variable; `/A/B:x` is `x` of the instance `A/B`, `/:x` the root's; a bare name the clip does not have falls back to the root's, and bare `PS2_*` names always live at the root (the engine's set).
